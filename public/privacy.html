Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 14, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Dashka Translator Lite collects the following information:

  • Voice input: Temporary audio data captured through your device's microphone for speech-to-text conversion. Speech recognition is performed locally on your device using Android's built-in Speech Recognition service. Audio recordings are not transmitted to our servers and are not retained after processing.
  • Text for translation: Text you type or speak is sent to our translation backend for processing. Text is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers after the translation request completes.
  • Translation history: Your translation history is stored locally on your device only and is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Usage analytics: Anonymous app performance data to improve user experience. No personal identifiers are collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

Text data is transmitted to third-party AI services (OpenAI and xAI) solely for the purpose of real-time translation and voice synthesis. We do not:

  • Retain text or voice data after processing
  • Sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes
  • Use data for advertising purposes
  • Build user profiles from translation content

3. Data Storage & Security

All personal data (translation history, user preferences) is stored locally on your device using secure Android storage mechanisms. We do not operate user databases or store personal information on backend servers. Translation requests are processed transiently and discarded after the response is delivered.

4. Third-Party Services

Dashka Translator Lite uses the following third-party services:

  • OpenAI API — used for text translation between Polish and Russian. Text is sent to OpenAI's servers for processing. See OpenAI Privacy Policy.
  • xAI Grok API — used for text-to-speech voice synthesis. Translated text is sent to xAI's servers to generate audio output. See xAI Privacy Policy.
  • Android Speech Recognition — used for voice input. Speech-to-text is performed on-device by the Android operating system. Refer to your device manufacturer's privacy policy for details on Android system services.

These third-party services have their own privacy policies and data handling practices. We do not control how these providers process data submitted through their APIs.

5. Permissions

RECORD_AUDIO: This permission is required to enable voice input functionality. Audio is captured only when you actively press the microphone button and is processed in real-time by Android's on-device speech recognition. No audio is stored or transmitted to our servers.

INTERNET: Required to send text to translation services and receive responses. No personal identifiers are included in requests.

6. Children's Privacy

Dashka Translator Lite is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us at the email below.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Clear your local translation history at any time through the app interface
  • Disable voice input features by denying the microphone permission
  • Uninstall the app to remove all locally stored data
  • Request information about data we hold about you (note: we do not maintain user accounts or databases)

Under GDPR (EU) and CCPA (California), you have additional rights including data access, correction, deletion, and portability. Since we do not store personal data on our servers, these rights are exercised by managing data directly on your device.

8. International Data Transfers

Translation requests may be processed by servers located outside your country of residence (primarily in the United States, where OpenAI and xAI operate). By using Dashka Translator Lite, you consent to the international transfer of text submitted for translation.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
